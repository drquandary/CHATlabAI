# Neuroaesthetics Glossary

> Terms and abbreviations used across the lab's work. CHATLabAI uses these to keep lit-review
> and writing consistent. Extend as the lab's vocabulary grows.

## Core concepts

- **Aesthetic experience** — The felt quality of perceiving something as beautiful, pleasing,
  or meaningful. Encompasses the phenomenal "feel" and the judgments that follow.
- **Aesthetic judgment** — An explicit decision or rating (e.g., "beautiful," "pleasing") about a
  stimulus, distinct from the underlying experience.
- **Aesthetic triad** — Chatterjee's framework: sensorimotor + emotion-valuation + meaning-knowledge
  systems jointly produce aesthetic experience. See `lab-info.md`.
- **Empirical aesthetics** — The experimental study of aesthetic experience and preference.
- **Neuroaesthetics** — The neuroscience of aesthetic experience; neural systems underlying
  beauty, art, and design perception.

## Stimulus domains

- **Beauty** — Canonical aesthetic positive; studied across face/scene/object/art beauty.
- **Face attractiveness** — Perceived beauty of faces; linked to averageness, symmetry, sexual
  dimorphism, gaze.
- **Art** — Representational and abstract; style, composition, framing, context effects.
- **Architecture / built form** — Spatial aesthetics, monumentality, proportion, wayfinding.
- **Design perception** — Functional aesthetics of objects and environments.

## Methods / terms

- **fMRI** — Functional magnetic resonance imaging; maps brain activity.
- **Lesion method** — Inference from deficits after focal brain injury.
- **VMPFC / OFC** — Ventromedial prefrontal cortex / orbitofrontal cortex; reward & valuation.
- **mOFC** — Medial orbitofrontal cortex; repeatedly implicated in beauty.
- **Fusiform face area (FFA)** — Ventral temporal region for face perception.
- **Parahippocampal place area (PPA)** — Scene/building perception.
- **Default mode network (DMN)** — Implicated in internally-generated, self-referential, and
  meaning-knowledge components.

## Abbreviations

- **PCfN** — Penn Center for Neuroaesthetics.
- **ENO** — "Eighty-neuron" / empirical neuroaesthetics organizing shorthand (lab-internal);
  use cautiously and only where established.
