# Neural Correlates of Face Beauty Judgment

## Abstract

This study examined the neural systems supporting aesthetic judgments of facial beauty.
Participants rated faces for attractiveness while undergoing fMRI. We expected activity in
the medial orbitofrontal cortex, consistent with prior work on reward and aesthetic
experience [@Chatterjee2003]. The results suggest that face beauty recruits valuation systems
that overlap with, but are distinct from, general reward processing. These findings are
consistent with the aesthetic triad framework.

## Introduction

The cognitive neuroscience of beauty has advanced considerably in recent decades. Early
lesion studies suggested that aesthetic experience depends on distributed neural systems
rather than a single "beauty center" [@Chatterjee2003]. Functional imaging work has since
identified regions within the medial orbitofrontal cortex that track the perceived beauty
of faces, scenes, and artworks. Here we ask whether face beauty specifically recruits
valuation systems. We review the evidence and report a replication.
